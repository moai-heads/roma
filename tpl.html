ROMA

a Caesar III style city builder · servant carts on real paths

  • Roads first. A building needs a road touching its edge, and that road must reach the Marketplace.
  • Servants path point-to-point. A cart spawns at the market, runs A* over the road network to that household's doorstep, delivers, then A*s back. Press G to see every route.
  • Water is a radius, not a cargo. Well = 6 tiles. Cistern = 12 but needs its own supply. Aqueduct = 18 but must touch a well or cistern. Chain them to push water across the map.
  • Clay needs water. Clay pits, iron mines, potteries and smithies all stall without a water radius. Woodcutters do not, and they slowly strip the soil around them.
  • Soil matters. Wheat needs 45% fertility, olives 60%. Press F for the overlay.
  • Ladder: food → pottery → tools → wine/luxury. Tent → Hovel → Domus → Townhouse → Mansion.
